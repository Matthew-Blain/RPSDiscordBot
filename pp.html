Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RPS Discord Bot ("the Bot") collects, uses and protects information when used through Discord.

Information We Collect

The Bot may process the following information:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Usernames and display names
  • Interactions with slash commands

How We Use Information

The information is used solely to:

  • Provide the Bot's features.
  • Respond to user commands.
  • Improve reliability and functionality.
  • Troubleshoot errors if necessary.

Data Storage

Unless explicitly stated by a specific feature, the Bot does not permanently store personal information. Any temporary data is used only to provide the requested functionality.

Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and is therefore subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Data Sharing

The Bot does not sell, rent or share personal information with third parties.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect any information processed by the Bot, however no electronic system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the Bot constitutes acceptance of any updated policy.
